Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some frequently asked questions about A.I, Robotics, and their development.

What is A.I(Artifical Intelligence)?

A.I, or Artifical Intelligence, is a technology that allow computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human thinking. Such as learning, problem solving, recognising patterns, and making decisions.

How do robots learn?

Robots can learn by using A.I and machinary. They can practice tasks consistently, receive feedback, and improve their behaviour over time.

Can robots develop themselves?

Some robotic operation systems can improve their own task performances through machine learning. However, this does not mean that physical robots can independently redesign and develop themselves.

What is machine learning?

Machine learning is a type of A.I in which computers learn patterns from data or experience, instead of running the same instructions for every possible situation.

What are the risks of A.I?

A.I can create challenges such as misinformation, fearmongering, privacy concerns, security risks, and unexpected behaviour. This is why responsible development and careful management matters.

Will robots replace humans?

Robots can still replace us in some repetitive or dangerous tasks. However, many jobs out there still require some sort of human creatitiy, communication, judgement, and decisions-making. The effect of robots will depend on how technology is developed and used.
